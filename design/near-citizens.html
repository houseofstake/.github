Q&A

A NEAR Verified Account gives you enhanced credibility and trust within the NEAR governance ecosystem. It ensures that each participant is a unique human, preventing Sybil attacks and ensuring fair representation in governance decisions.
You need a valid NEAR wallet and a government-issued ID document. You must also be able to complete a liveness check using your device camera.
Accepted documents include national ID cards, passports, and driver's licenses issued by your country. The document must be valid and not expired.
Yes. Your personal data is processed by our identity verification partner and is never stored on-chain. Only a cryptographic proof of your uniqueness is associated with your wallet.
You can still use NEAR, but certain governance features and Citizens House participation may require a verified account. Verification can be completed at any time.
